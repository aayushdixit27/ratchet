## Why it broke, and it is not a bug
Real Pioneer prices are roughly twenty times lower than our fixture estimates. At those prices the cold-versus-warm gap collapses from 14× to about 3×, and with four bugs per iteration the per-iteration variance is larger than the signal. **The effect is real; the dollar figure is just too small and too noisy to carry a five-point chart.**

## The fix: change the hero metric to LLM CALLS PER VERIFIED FIX
Cost is downstream of whatever a vendor charges this week. **Calls are an invariant.** Cold is always 4 calls, warm is always 1. As warm share climbs 20% → 100%, calls per fix falls 3.3 → 1.0, cleanly and monotonically, on live data with real model names.

This is not a retreat. It is a sharper claim, and it is exactly the line already in `POSITIONING.md`:
> **"Pioneer makes every call cheaper. We delete the call."**

Cost becomes the *second* number, stated as a range with Pioneer's own savings telemetry beside it. The two savings stack and we can now show both honestly.
