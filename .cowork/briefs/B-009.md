## Four gotchas that will cost you time if you don't know them

1. **`ENGINE_NOT_INITIALIZED` is expected right after container start.** Retry with backoff; do not treat it as a failure and degrade.
2. **Dynamic field-index creation is NOT implemented.** Declare filterable payload fields via `PayloadSchema` **at collection creation time**. We filter on `verified`, `bug_class`, `origin_org` — declare all three up front or we can't filter later without recreating the collection.
3. **VectorAI DB ships no embedding model.** We bring our own, and `<DIM>` must exactly match what `embed()` produces. A mismatch raises a gRPC-wrapped error that is *not* a connection problem — the guide is explicit that only `UNAVAILABLE`/timeout means connectivity.
4. **Community Edition caps at 5,000 vectors across all collections**, checked by a background job — writes succeed and then start failing with no warning at insert time. Our corpus is dozens of patterns, so we're nowhere near it, but if writes start failing mysteriously that's the first thing to check. Deletes still work while over the cap.

Not implemented server-side, don't try: grouped search, collection aliases. RRF/DBSF fusion **is** available but runs client-side in the SDK (`reciprocal_rank_fusion`, `distribution_based_score_fusion`) — that's documented behaviour, not a bug.

## Embeddings — a judgement call, time-boxed
Our current `embed()` gives same-class 0.78–0.80 vs nearest non-match 0.42, which works. Actian's guide recommends **`sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`, 384d, cosine** as the default.

**Do this in two steps.** First get Actian storing and searching with our existing `embed()`, dimension-matched — that is a genuine integration and it protects the demo. *Then*, if you have spare time, try MiniLM behind a flag and compare separation on the same bug set. Real sentence embeddings make the cross-app transfer claim much more credible to a technical judge than hand-rolled similarity does. **Do not swap it in without re-checking the warm threshold** — a different embedding space means 0.60 means something different.
